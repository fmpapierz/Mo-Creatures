46 creatures. One of the most legendary mods ever made.
DrZhark's Mo'Creatures — rebuilt from the ground up for Minecraft 26.2, on both Fabric and NeoForge.

⚠️ Read This First — This Is A Giant Port In Active Development

Mo'Creatures was built for Minecraft 1.12.2. Bringing it to 26.2 meant rewriting essentially the entire mod — every entity, model, renderer, item, block, the dimension, the spawning system, and the save format — against a Minecraft that has changed almost beyond recognition in the years since.

All 46 creatures, 182 items, 14 blocks and the Wyvern Lair are in and working on both loaders. But a port of this size will have bugs, and some of the deeper 1.12 behaviours are still being rebuilt. I'd rather tell you that up front than have you find out the hard way.

💬 So here's my ask: tell me when something breaks. If a creature misbehaves, a texture looks wrong, something crashes, or it just doesn't feel like the Mo'Creatures you remember — report it and I will fix it fast. Bug reports are the single quickest path to a solid mod, and I genuinely want them. Nothing is too small.

✨ What This Mod Does

Mo'Creatures fills the Minecraft world with life. 46 creatures across land, sea and sky — tame them, breed them, ride them, name them, carry them in your pocket, and fight the ones that fight back. It adds a custom dimension, a full set of weapons and armour, and one of the deepest horse systems ever put in a Minecraft mod. This is that mod, running natively on modern Minecraft.

🌟 Features

  • 🦌 21 land animals — bear, big cat, bird, boar, crocodile, deer, duck, elephant, fox, goat, horse, kitty, komodo, mouse, ostrich, pet scorpion, snake, turkey, turtle, wyvern, bunny
  • 🐬 5 aquatic creatures — dolphin, fishy, jellyfish, ray, shark
  • 🦋 5 flying insects — bee, butterfly, dragonfly, firefly, fly
  • 🐌 5 ground bugs — crab, maggot, snail, cricket, roach
  • 👹 10 monsters — golem, ogre, rat, hell rat, scorpion, wild wolf, werewolf, wraith, flame wraith, undead horse
  • 🎨 Sub-type variants — 31 of the 46 creatures roll their own skin/colour on spawn, so no two look alike
  • 🐴 Horses with coat inheritance — breed two coats and the foal inherits, with a chance to mutate
  • 🏹 Tame, breed, ride, name — the core Mo'Creatures loop, intact
  • 🔮 Pet Amulet — capture a tamed creature, carry it, release it anywhere with its type and owner preserved
  • 🌌 The Wyvern Lair — a custom floating-island dimension, reached with the Staff of Portal
  • ⚔️ 182 items — katana, sai, nunchaku, bo, shark sword, silver sword, scorpion weapons, fur/hide/reptile/scorpion armour sets, foods and more
  • 🧱 14 blocks — the Wyvern Lair and Ogre Lair building sets
  • 🔊 296 authentic sound files / 161 sound events — extracted straight from the original release. It sounds right.
  • 🔀 Fabric AND NeoForge — one shared codebase, identical content and behaviour on both

📖 Getting Started

  1. Install Architectury API (required on both loaders), plus Fabric API if you're on Fabric.
  2. Drop the jar in your mods folder and launch.
  3. Open the creative inventory and find the Mo'Creatures tab (the icon is a Bunny spawn egg).
  4. Or just go exploring — creatures spawn naturally throughout the Overworld.

The essentials:

  • 🥕 Taming: right-click a creature holding its food. Bunny → carrot, horse → wheat, turtle → melon slice, roach → rotten flesh.
  • 🐎 Riding: tame a horse, right-click it with a saddle, then right-click with an empty hand. Look to steer, W to go.
  • 🏷️ Naming: rename a Medallion in an anvil, then right-click your tamed pet with it.
  • 🔮 Amulet: right-click a tamed creature with the Pet Amulet to store it; right-click the air to release it.
  • 🌌 Wyvern Lair: right-click with the Staff of Portal to travel there, and again to come home.

📦 Requirements

  • Minecraft 26.2
  • Architectury API (both loaders)
  • Fabric: Fabric Loader 0.19.3+ and Fabric API
  • NeoForge: 26.2.0.7-beta or newer
  • Java 25 (required by Minecraft 26.x itself)

🚧 What's Not Rebuilt Yet (So You Don't Report It As A Bug)

Being straight with you about where the port stands. Everything below is known, on the list, and getting worked on — these aren't bugs, they're the parts I haven't reached yet:

  • 🍖 Taming food is wired for bunny, horse, turtle and roach so far. Other species spawn and behave correctly but can't be tamed by feeding yet — use creative in the meantime.
  • 🎒 Most mount inventories (chest and armour slots) aren't wired up yet. Riding works; the storage GUI mostly doesn't.
  • 🪄 Some special items are cosmetic for now — the Whip, Nether Cannon, Staff of Teleport, Builder's Hammer, scrolls, key and CreaturePedia are registered with their real art but don't have their legacy effects back yet.
  • 🐴 Horse genetics is simplified to coat inheritance plus a mutation chance. The full hybrid table — zorse, mule, zonky — and the white fairy aren't back yet.
  • 🦓 Wild zebras and donkeys don't spawn naturally yet.
  • 🐻 Biome-picked variants (polar bear, snow leopard, snow fox, cave/nether/frost scorpions) can't roll naturally yet — their art ships, but the biome selection isn't reconnected.
  • 🌌 The Wyvern Lair terrain currently generates from vanilla floating-island blocks rather than the mod's own lair grass/dirt/stone, so it doesn't have its signature look yet. The sky, biome, trees and wyverns are all correct.
  • ✨ A few signature visual effects are missing — the firefly's glowing belly, the jellyfish's translucency and bell-pulse, the wraiths' ghostly transparency.
  • ⚙️ The F9 settings screen and /moc config save correctly, but a number of the behaviour toggles aren't consulted by gameplay yet.
  • 🚪 No buildable portal frame — the Wyvern Lair is reached with the Staff of Portal item instead.
  • 🛡️ Worn armour uses the vanilla body overlay. The inventory icons are the authentic original art.

Anything not on this list that looks broken almost certainly is broken — please report it!

❓ FAQ

Q: Is this the real Mo'Creatures?
A: This is DrZhark's Mo'Creatures, ported to Minecraft 26.2. Same creatures, same art, same sounds, same mod — running on a version of Minecraft it was never written for. See the credits below; DrZhark made this mod, I only carried it forward.

Q: Fabric or NeoForge?
A: Both, from one codebase. Content and behaviour are identical — pick whichever your pack uses.

Q: Do I need Architectury API?
A: Yes, on both loaders. That's what lets one codebase run on each.

Q: Is it stable enough to actually play?
A: Yes — it boots clean on both loaders with no missing textures or models, and the whole tame/breed/ride/capture loop works. But it's a huge port under active development, so play it expecting rough edges, and please report them.

Q: Will the missing features come back?
A: That's the plan. The list above is my roadmap. What people actually ask for gets prioritised, so speak up.

Q: Can I use this in a modpack?
A: Please ask first — Mo'Creatures is DrZhark's work and its licensing isn't mine to hand out.

Q: Does it work on a server?
A: It's designed to run on both sides. Report anything that doesn't — server-side issues are especially useful to hear about.
